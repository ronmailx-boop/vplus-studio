1. Our Commitment to Accessibility

Vplus Studio places great importance on providing an equal, respectful, and accessible service to the general public, including people with disabilities. We work to make our website accessible in accordance with the requirements of the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility Adjustments), 5773-2013, the Israeli Standard IS 5568 (based on the WCAG 2.1 guidelines at level AA), and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998.

2. Accessibility Measures Implemented on the Website

Among other things, the following measures have been implemented on the Website to improve accessibility:

  • Semantic structure: use of semantic HTML tags (headings, paragraphs, links) that enable proper navigation via assistive technologies (such as screen readers).
  • Color contrast: a color palette (light text on a dark background, with prominent accent-colored action buttons) was chosen with the aim of meeting appropriate contrast ratios in accordance with WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines.
  • Bilingual support: the Website is available in English (default) and Hebrew, including full support for right-to-left (RTL) text direction when switching to Hebrew.
  • Responsive, mobile-first design: the Website is designed for comfortable viewing across a wide range of screen sizes and devices, including mobile phones.
  • Clear links: contact links (such as the email link) are phrased clearly and identifiably.
